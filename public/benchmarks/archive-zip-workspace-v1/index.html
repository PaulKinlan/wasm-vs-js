Build, list and extract a ZIP workspace

No performance claim. The default bounded mode creates and lists 1,000 entries, extracts four selected paths, and checks their structure and contents without a frozen output oracle. The full mode creates and lists exactly 10,000 entries, extracts ten frozen paths, and checks three exact SHA-256 outputs plus every target-specific counter. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Bounded mode stops after 10 seconds. Full mode checks the three frozen SHA-256 outputs and every counter in the target-specific output manifest before reporting success, and stops after 30 seconds. Zip64 is forbidden because the full fixture has fewer than 65,535 entries and every size and offset is below 2³².
