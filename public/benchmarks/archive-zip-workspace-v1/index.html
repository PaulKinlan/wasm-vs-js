What it does

ZIP workspace validation: creates and lists entries, extracts frozen paths, and checks exact SHA-256 outputs + target-specific counters.

Why compare Wasm vs JS

ZIP entry framing, CRC/SHA hashing, and extraction over thousands of entries are exactly where a linear-memory Wasm target differs from the JavaScript engine.

Fixed contract

Bounded mode creates and lists 1,000 entries, extracts four selected paths, and checks their structure and contents without a frozen output oracle. Full mode creates and lists exactly 10,000 entries, extracts ten frozen paths, and checks three exact SHA-256 outputs plus every target-specific counter. Zip64 is forbidden because every size and offset is below 2³². This candidate does not count toward the frozen catalog denominator until independent acceptance.
