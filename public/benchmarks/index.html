Rules for filling the catalog

A fixture can move to frozen only after its rights, provenance, redistribution terms, and SHA-256 have been checked. When results arrive:

  • algorithm-equivalent aggregates may contain only one declared algorithm family;
  • semantic product-choice comparisons stay separate from controlled algorithm comparisons;
  • load, transfer, compute, IO, rendering, memory, boundary, and end-to-end costs remain separate fields;
  • missing JavaScript, linear-Wasm, WasmGC, or hybrid cells retain a typed reason.
