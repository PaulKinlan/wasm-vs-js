What it does

Renders the frozen Cornell-box scene with a scalar BVH, four-bounce paths, strict-f32, tone mapping, and a complete RGBA oracle.

Why compare Wasm vs JS

BVH traversal, ray-triangle intersection, and strict-f32 pixel accumulation are where a linear-memory Wasm target genuinely differs from the JavaScript engine.

Fixed contract

One thread performs complete ray generation, fixed-order BVH traversal, Lambert integration and framebuffer quantization. Canvas presentation happens after validation. Threads, SharedArrayBuffer and SIMD are separate unavailable variants.
