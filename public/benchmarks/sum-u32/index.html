Multi-Language Comparison

The same modulo-2³² sum kernel written in JavaScript, hand-written WAT, AssemblyScript, C, C++, Rust, and Dart (WasmGC), compared side by side. All variants are verified bit-identical to the frozen oracle (sum 145417951). The Dart kernel lives in fft_kernel.dart and exports sum_u32 on the shared WasmGC module. Sources: sum_u32.ts · sum.wat · sum_u32.c · sum_u32.cpp · sum_u32.rs · fft_kernel.dart.
