Each fresh launch measures both exact variants in the frozen balanced order. A pair enters timing summaries only after both records pass output, fixed-work, source/artifact, cache, provenance, and exact cleanup checks. Partial pairs, failures, and blocked launches remain denominator rows but never become timing data. There are no substitutions or automatic retries.

Stopping and uncertainty

Precision is checked only at 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60 committed pairs in each stratum. Five sequential looks use conservative Bonferroni-protected two-sided 99% bootstrap intervals. The paired estimator is the median per-launch log ratio of the Wasm median to the JavaScript median, using 10,000 deterministic paired resamples.

A stratum stops when its multiplicative confidence-interval half-width is at most 3%. Reaching 60 pairs without that precision is inconclusive, never a win, loss, pass, or zero.

Exact scope

  • Google Chrome for Testing 150.0.7871.24 with exact binary SHA-256 and ordinary cache enabled.
  • Exact local origin, source commit, benchmark, build manifest, input, oracle, JavaScript, and Wasm hashes.
  • Cold profiles have no priming, Service Worker, or origin storage; warm profiles prime only the three exact assets.
  • Headline launches are uninstrumented: no profiler, trace, heap snapshot, forced GC, or memory polling.
  • CDP, process-memory, tiering, and heap diagnostics require separate launches and a separate permit; they cannot enter headline cells.
  • Unavailable evidence remains typed with a reason and is never changed to numeric zero.
