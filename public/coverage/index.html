What every page measures — and what it does not

Each benchmark page can produce evidence at up to four scopes, and can compare up to seven language implementations of its kernel. Almost none produce all of it. This page reads the shipped pages and manifests directly and reports the gaps, because a gap that is not listed reads as coverage.

Artifact provenance

Every compiled kernel that a workload manifest declares now has a recorded build recipe — the exact command, the source hash and the artifact hash. Not every recipe reproduces the committed binary yet: those artifacts were built before the recipe was recorded, by means the repository does not capture. The shortfall is counted here rather than left implied.
