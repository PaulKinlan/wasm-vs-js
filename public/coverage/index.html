What every page measures — and what it does not

Each benchmark page can produce evidence at up to four scopes, and can compare up to seven language implementations of its kernel. Almost none produce all of it. This page reads the shipped pages and manifests directly and reports the gaps, because a gap that is not listed reads as coverage.
