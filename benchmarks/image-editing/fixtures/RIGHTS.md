# Generated fixture rights

`generated-map-64x48.rgba` and `generated-photo-40x30.rgba` are emitted solely by `benchmarks/image-editing/fixtures.ts`. The generator reads no photographs, fonts, icons, or other third-party media.

The repository contributors dedicate the generated RGBA bytes to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (`SPDX-License-Identifier: CC0-1.0`). The source generator remains covered by the repository's MIT license.

`fixture-manifest.json` records the generator path and hash, fixed seeds, byte layout, dimensions, and SHA-256 for each generated file.
